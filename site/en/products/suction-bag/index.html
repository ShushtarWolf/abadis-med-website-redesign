1 Liter Single-Use Disposable Suction Liner Bag

Volume:1 liter

Characteristic: Single-use (disposable) design, Contains gel powder, hydrophobic filter ( flow stopper filter), suitable with all suction devices, has central suction protection system

Types of filter: Filters used in Abadis single-use suction liner bags are produced in 3 types that prevent liquids from overflowing.

Row Filter Name Description
1 Hydrophobic filter Prevent microorganisms and bacteria from entering the suction source and the surrounding environment
2 Dual filter Prevents the reduction of pressure drop in the surgery that use the electrocautery device.
3 mechanical filter It has the lowest pressure drop among existing filters
