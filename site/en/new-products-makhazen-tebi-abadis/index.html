New products of Abadis Med

New products of Abadis Med

Abadis Med as the largest manufacturer of disposable suction liner bags in Iran, recently unveiled two new products called wall suction and regulator.

The regulator is one of the new products of this company for the first time in Iran. The regulator is a piece between the vacuum output and the suction canister by which the amount of vacuum can be adjusted. This regulator has a beautiful design and high quality construction.

Find out more

Wall suction is responsible for setting the central vacuum, so it is also called regulatory suction. How this device works is that it is installed on the splint by a vacuum adapter and the required amount of vacuum can be adjusted by this device.
