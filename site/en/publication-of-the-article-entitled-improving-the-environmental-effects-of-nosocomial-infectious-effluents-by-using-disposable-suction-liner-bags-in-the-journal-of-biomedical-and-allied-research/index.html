Publication of an article by Abadis in ISI journal

The article titled “ Improving The Environmental Effects Of Nosocomial Infectious Effluents By Using Disposable Suction Liner Bags” was published in the Journal of Biomedical And Allied Research on October 16, 2023. This article was written by Niloufar Sharafi, Navid Hasanali and Alireza Hasanali.
This article was presented by Niloufar Sharafi in the 10th Congress of Infection Control which was held in December 2022 in the workshop of Abadis Med. This presentation examined and compared permanent with disposable suction liner bags in terms of environmental impact, infection control, costs, and other characteristics.
Abadis Med, as a knowledge-based company, congratulates the company’s directors as well as Ms. Sharafi for the publication of this article in the ISI Journal.
