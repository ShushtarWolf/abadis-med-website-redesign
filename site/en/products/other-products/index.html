Disposable suction toothbrush

The disposable suction toothbrush is used for patients who cannot keep up their own oral hygiene. It connects to the suction unit through the suction tube, and suction runs while the teeth are brushed. Regular use has a lasting health benefit.

Endotracheal tube holder

The fixer (endotracheal tube holder) holds the tracheal tube in a suitable position in the patient’s mouth and makes the patient more comfortable.

Features

  • Safe operation
  • Easy to use
  • Adjustable strap
  • Supports hospital infection control
  • Avoids sores on the lips and mouth
  • Removes the cost of gauze and disinfectants
  • More comfortable for the patient

It is designed for tracheal tubes with an internal diameter (I.D.) of 6.0 to 9.0 mm and an external diameter (O.D.) of 8.7 to 12.7 mm. The tube must fall inside the holder’s range.

Disposable kidney dish

The disposable kidney dish is a shallow, graduated, kidney-shaped bowl used in hospitals, clinics and dental and aesthetic practices for:

  • Holding samples taken from patients for laboratory examination
  • Holding and moving surgical instruments during a procedure
  • Holding patients’ dressings
  • Washing surgical instruments with sterile water
  • Keeping instruments sterile
  • Making counting easier
  • Storing instruments when they are not in use

Advantages

  • Single use
  • Light, and easy to carry
  • Autoclavable
  • Better hospital infection control

Material

  • New polypropylene plastic, and it can be autoclaved

Capacity

  • 600 ml

Carton: 35.5 × 24.5 × 49 cm. Quantity: 400 per carton.

Splash-Vac

No separate photo of this product is in the archived site

Splash-Vac is a floor suction device for collecting fluids, hair, fat and pathological waste from the floor in sterile rooms such as the operating room, surgical clinics and intensive care. Connected to the central or a portable suction system, it clears the floor quickly without hands in the waste and stops contamination spreading around the surgical site.

Advantages

  • Clears a large volume of fluid from an operating-room or clinic floor quickly and completely
  • Flexible hose
  • Connects to central or portable suction
