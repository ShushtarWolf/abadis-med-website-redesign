Suction canisters

The suction canister is a polycarbonate jar that holds a disposable suction bag.

Uses

Volume

1 litre, 2 litres, 3 litres

Material

Transparent polycarbonate

Features

Resistant to breakage, washable and autoclavable, light, and graduated.

Wall suction

Wall suction can be used in any part of the hospital that has a central vacuum.

Components

Wall suction includes one metal regulator, a filter that shuts off when fluid passes, a suction canister with connectors, and a wall clamp for mounting on a console.

Features

  • The main parts are metal and brass.
  • An analog gauge marked in bar and mmHg.
  • A REG/OFF push button to connect or disconnect the vacuum quickly during use.
  • Impact resistant, and it does not break over long use.
  • A knob sets the vacuum and reaches the maximum in 1.5 turns.
  • Accepts the connectors used to join wall suction to the outlet.
  • Working range 0 to −1 bar.
  • Nickel-chrome plating, so fluids and air do not shorten its life.
  • No diaphragm, so the unit lasts longer.
  • Straightforward to use.
  • Five-year warranty on functional failure.

Collection cup

No separate photo of this product is in the archived site

The Abadis collection cup collects and separates particles in suction fluid. A sample can be taken without touching the collected fluid, which avoids contamination, leaks and sampling errors.

Advantages

  • Collects particles from fluid drained during medical procedures.
  • A separator that keeps the user away from the fluid and the particles.
  • Single use, for safety, less contamination and infection-control practice.

Uses

  • Operating room
  • Sampling fluids that contain particles
  • Collecting a sample for laboratory and pathology examination
  • Hospital suction systems that use a disposable suction bag

Fat collection canister system

No separate photo of this product is in the archived site

The Abadis fat-collection system connects to a liposuction unit and collects the harvested fat in a closed, graduated canister. After liposuction and fat preparation, the canister outlet is connected to the unit’s injection pump and the fat is transferred to the recipient site through the dedicated hose and cannula. Harvesting and reinjection are then one workflow in the same procedure.

Features

  • Connects to the injection pump of the liposuction unit
  • Transfers fat in a closed, controlled circuit
  • For transferring and injecting fat after liposuction
  • Fewer manual transfers of fat between containers
  • Limits contact between the harvested fat and the room
  • Injection flow is controlled from the unit’s pump
  • Keeps harvesting, preparation and injection in one sequence
