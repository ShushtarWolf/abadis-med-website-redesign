Wall Suction

Wall suction can be used in any part of the hospital that has a central vacuum.

Product Components:

Wall suction includes a   regulators, a fluid-blocking filter, a suction canister with fittings and a wall bracket for mounting on the console.

 

Product Features:

  •  The main components of wall suction are made of metal and brass.
  • It has a hand display with BAR and mmHg parameters.
  • It has a REG / OFF push button to quickly turn the vacuum on or off during use.
  • Impact resistance and no breakage of the product during long operation
  • Has a volume to adjust the desired amount of vacuum and reach the maximum possible vacuum with only 1.5 revolutions
  • Supports all available connectors to connect wall suction to splint
  • Has a negative interval of 0 to 1 times
  • Has nickel-chrome plating to increase product life span against fluids and air
  • No aperture to extend product life span
  • Easy to use 
  • Has a five-year  warranty

Collection Cup

The sample collection cup is a key accessory for medical suction systems, designed to collect body fluid samples before they reach the disposable suction bag. It enables accurate and sterile sample separation during suction without cross-contamination from other waste fluids.

The airtight and sterile design of the cup ensures samples remain uncontaminated and ready for diagnostic or laboratory analysis.

 

Product Features:

  • Sterile and contamination-free collection of suctioned body fluids

  • Easy integration with standard suction systems and disposable suction liners

  • Maintains sample sterility until it reaches the lab

  • Leakproof and durable design to prevent air or fluid leakage

  • Ideal for use in operating rooms, ICUs, CCUs, clinics, and diagnostic labs

Fat Collection Canister System

The Abadis Fat Collection Canister System is designed to connect to a liposuction unit and collect harvested adipose tissue in a closed, graduated reservoir. After liposuction and fat preparation are completed, the reservoir outlet is connected to the injection pump of the liposuction unit. The prepared fat is then transferred to the recipient site through dedicated medical tubing and an injection cannula. This allows fat harvesting and reinjection to be performed within an integrated workflow during the same surgical session.

Product Features

  • Connects to the injection pump of a liposuction unit
  • Transfers fat through a closed and controlled circuit
  • Suitable for fat transfer and injection following liposuction
  • Reduces manual transfers and the need to move fat between multiple containers
  • Limits the exposure of harvested fat to the surrounding environment
  • Enables injection-flow control through the device pump
  • Supports continuity throughout the fat harvesting, preparation, and injection process
