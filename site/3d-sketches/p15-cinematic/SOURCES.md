# P15 — Plastic suction canister clamp (wall-mounted holder)

## Product identity
Official FA: گیره مخزن (پلاستیکی) / گیره مخزن دیواری.
EN: Plastic suction canister clamp / Wall-Mounted Suction Canister Holder.
Product code 060503. Inventory P15.

Official catalog (drawing / website schematic): width 4.6 cm, length 8.7 cm, weight 11.5 g. Depth is not printed on the official drawing; 18 mm is an estimate carried from Hamid's Grok design and kept because it agrees with the official product photographs.

## Geometry source — reconstructed, not original factory CAD
Hamid supplied a Grok-built design at https://crane-blaze-orbit-eagle.grok.me (the “ABADIS” part). That page generated an in-browser mesh (`abadis-87x26mm.glb` / `.stl`) from an extruded 26 × 87 × 18 mm front profile with side steps and recessed ABADIS lettering.

Grok's “87 × 26 mm” misread the official overall width. 87 mm length is correct, but 26 mm is only the ABADIS front-face width, not the official 4.6 cm overall width. The official set photograph (clamp + rear part) shows a wider C-frame back plate and two mounting holes that the extruded 26 mm block omitted (the official 3/4 product shot hides the holes).

This mesh is therefore a reconstruction: Hamid's Grok front profile, side steps and ABADIS lettering were kept and corrected to the official photographs and drawing.

- Overall extents: 46 × 87 × 18 mm (W × L × D). Depth 18 mm is estimated.
- C-frame back plate, left lip, closed top and bottom rails, and two through-holes: from the official set photograph.
- Front body as a rounded “pillow” column (~24.5 mm wide) with a shallow mid-height side step (about 3 mm inset, from the official set-photo silhouette) and a recessed rectangular ABADIS plaque with raised/embossed letters: from the official photographs and the Grok letter outlines.
- Provenance label, used on the cinematic and the sketch: “reconstructed from Hamid's Grok design, corrected to official photos/drawing”.

No invented mounting hardware. The genuine P05 canister collar-mount hook is about 52 mm wide, while this clamp's pocket is about 16 mm; a simple slide-in engagement is not clear from the CAD, so the cinematic does not show an assembled canister.

A metal/white C-channel photograph on the site (`5eb6129757a4`) is a different (metal) variant and was not used as the source of truth for this plastic part.
