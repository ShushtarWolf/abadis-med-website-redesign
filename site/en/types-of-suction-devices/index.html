Types of Suction Devices

Types of Suction Devices

  • Central suction
  • Portable suction (portable)

In centers with central suction , there is a vacuum pump in the engine room equipment of this center, which produces vacuum chambers . this vacuum is piped to the operating rooms by antibacterial copper pipes and is used

Portable suction

Portable suction cups are portable and can be easily moved to the desired location. Therefore, this type of suction is used in homes , ambulances and clinics and some hospitals. Examples of portable suction are as follows

  • Single glass electric suction
  • Rechargeable single glass suction (ambulance)
  • Suction of two long glasses
  • Short glass suction
  • Manual suction
  • Pedal suction

Electric single glass suction and rechargeable single glass suction (ambulance): single glass suction with a one -liter tank is used to collect mucus and other patient secretions, especially for lung patients, and is generally for home use. Rechargeable single glass (ambulance) suctin single glass electric suction can also be charged. The important thing about a suction device is that the material inside the glass should never be more than half full and the glass should be emptied and washed.

Long and short glass suction: This type of suction is generally used in hospitals and dental clinics because it has two tanks without collecting suction materials. The term long and short suction is derived from the appearance of suction.

Important technical parameters in the purchase of medical suctions

-suction capacity (liters per minute) and high engine quality and suction

– negative pressure of maximum relative vacuum ( load. mmHg . kpa or cm of water)

-Have the necessary approvals such as (FDA) or markings such as (CE) or required standards

-high output capacity and power

-No sound and vibration

-Low maintenance costs

-Irrigation system or as an option

– High sterility of the body

-High volume of suction tanks

-Duration and method of after-sales service and warranty

– The price is commensurate with the performance of the device

– The pipe material of the device is anti-spark and made of materias that do not fold over each other and do not cause clogging after working for a while.
