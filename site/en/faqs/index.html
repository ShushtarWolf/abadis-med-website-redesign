Technical guide

Should the Abadis canisters be changed after each use ?

No, Abadis canisters do not need to be replaced after each use.

What material are the Abadis Suction Bags made of?

The disposable suction liner bag is made of polyethylene (PE) and the canisters are made of polycarbonate.

What is the function of Abadis suction bag flow stopper filter?

These filters are made of nanoparticles and polymer and composite materials and  Abadis med Company is introduced as the first manufacturer of these filters in Iran.

Are the flow-stoppers filters produced in Iran?

Abadis Medical canisters Company is the first manufacturer of these filters in Iran.

What is a Abadis solidifying agent?

Absorbent powder in Abadis suction bags causes jelly of liquid materials inside the bags, which makes it easy to carry and also in case of overflow or torn bag, causes better collection of liquids inside the bag.

How long should suction bags be replaced when they start to be used?

Suction bags can be replaced after full filling and be sure not to move the suction bags of different patients together to better control the infection.

In which case disposable suction bags are not covered by the warranty?

1) Don’t use a disposable suction bag that fits the size of the canister.

2) suction Bags shouldn’t be replaced at least once every 24 hours if used.

3) Use the suction bags more than once.

This product will be out of warranty if the Abadis disposable suction bag isn’t used properly in the above cases.

What is the correct way to wash Abadis suction canisters?

1) Use disinfectants and water in the ratio of 1 to 10 to wash the suction canisters.

2) Roll the suction canisters for 10 minutes in the solution mentioned above; Be careful not to put the canisters in solution for more than the mentioned time because it will damage the canisters.

3)after that place the canister on a  dry towel and allow the canisters to dry completely.

4)Abadis suction canisters can be washed in autoclave up to 120 ° C.

5)The canister must be replaced if there are any cracks or fractures.

How to wash the canisters is only acceptable as above, and if the washing is not done in this way, it will be out of warranty.

* Keep in mind that Abadis suction canisters only need to be washed if there is contamination.

What is the lifespan of an antibacterial filter?

When the paper inside the antibacterial filter changes color, the filter must be replaced, and if the filter is changed.

What is the life span of the flow stopperfilter?

flow stopper filter should be replaced when fluids penetrate the filter surface or the suction pressure drops, and it is best to replace the circuit breaker filter every month for proper operation.

Are the wall and portable brackets washable?

Yes, wall and portable brackets in both aluminum and plastic can be washed up to a maximum temperature of 95 degrees.

In what case is the trolley out of warranty?

Abadis trolley is out of warranty in case of severe impact.

In what case will Abadis suction canisters be out of warranty?

Abadis suction canister will be out of warranty if it is  damaged.

In what case is the Abadis regulator out of warranty?

Abadis regulator will be out of warranty if it is hit and the device is damaged.

Installation

How do you install Abadis Suction Bags?
  1. According to the shape of the silicone tube, we connect it from the regulator or suction device to the T connector on the canister.
  2. On the other hand, connect the T connector silicone tube to the connection of the connection and connect the connection on the vacuum part on the door.
  3. Connect the suction tube (suction tube) to the patient (patient) part and perform the suction operation from the suction tube (suction tube).

What points should be considered when installing Abadis suction bags to prevent pressure drop and leakage?
  1. From the connection of T connector on the canister
  2. Connection and non-leakage in silicone hoses related to suction device and T connector
  3. Connecting the suction tube to the patient
Do Abadis suction bags can be series together?

Yes, you can see the following image:

  1. According to the picture of the silicone tube, we connect it from the regulator or suction device to the Tconnector on the canister with the initial suction bag (pre).
  2. On the other hand, we connect the T connector of silicone tube to the next canister, which contains the Final (or final) bag and has a flow stopper filter.
  3. On the other hand, we connect the Tconnector of the same canister with a silicone tube to the vacuum part on the door.
  4. From the patient’s connection on the lid of the same canister, we connect it to the vacuum part of the previous canister.
  5. Connect the suction tube (suction tube) to the patient (patient) part and perform the suction operation from the suction tube (suction tube).
How many suction bags can be series together?

Up to four canisters can be connected in a series.
