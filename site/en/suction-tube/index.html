Suction tube

The Abadis suction tube is an essential part of a medical suction system. It removes blood, secretions and fluids during surgery and treatment. It improves the surgeon’s view and, by carrying fluid away quickly, helps stop contamination and infection spreading to other parts of the body.

Uses

  • Keeps the wound clear so the bleeding site can be seen and infection is not transferred
  • Drains and cleans abscess cavities
  • Used in chest, abdominal and internal-cavity surgery
  • Suitable for removing damaged brain tissue or soft tumours
  • Helps compress a bleeding vessel
  • Used in aesthetic procedures: liposuction, lipolaser and lipomatic
  • Suitable for dental clinics
  • Used with a fingertip, a suction catheter and other related devices

Features and advantages

  • Medical-grade PVC
  • Sterile, for the operating room and specialist wards
  • Two models: transparent (the fluid path stays visible) and ribbed (resists kinking and blockage)
  • Controlled flexibility, to reduce injury to sensitive tissue
  • A secure, leak-free connection to suction equipment
  • Compatible with hospital and clinic suction systems
  • A smooth inner surface, to resist clogging and keep flow even

Size, colours and custom orders

  • Three standard sizes: 25 blue, 30 grey, 35 black
  • Standard length: 2 metres
  • Other lengths and special sizes can be made for a centre

Packaging

  • Die-cut packs of 25
  • 100 pieces per carton
  • Carton: 58 × 42 × 41 cm

Fingertip (suction connector)

The fingertip (suction connector) is a small part of a medical suction system that lets staff control the suction level precisely. It sits between the suction tube and other suction devices such as a Nelaton catheter or a suction catheter.

It is used in general, plastic, neurosurgical and dental procedures. It improves suction and reduces injury to sensitive tissue.

Features and advantages

  • Suction is controlled by placing or lifting a finger on the port
  • Ergonomic shape for long use
  • Less risk of injury to soft tissue during suction
  • Fits suction tubes
  • A secure connection that does not leak
  • Suction is adjusted without switching the unit off and on
  • Medical-grade, for general surgery, dentistry and intensive care

Material and size

The fingertip is medical polyvinyl chloride (PVC), in a large and a small size to match suction tubes.
