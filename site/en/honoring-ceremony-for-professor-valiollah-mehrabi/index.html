Honoring Ceremony for Professor Valiollah Mehrabi


Dr. Valiollah Mehrabi once said, “The one who works with their hands is a creator, the one who works with their mind is a scientist, and the one who works with their heart is an artist. A surgeon is someone who uses their hands, mind, and heart to help humanity.”

Dr. Valiollah Mehrabi was born in Iran in 1937. Dr. Mehrabi embarked on his journey in medicine with a dream of studying at Tehran University’s Medical School. Although his dream was initially deferred, he pursued his medical education in Istanbul, Turkey. Before boarding the bus to Turkey, he made a promise to himself in front of the gates of Tehran University: if he couldn’t enter as a student today, one day he would return as a professor. Dr. Mehrabi completed his medical studies in Turkey and furthered his expertise in Germany. There, he studied under some of the most renowned surgeons of the time at St. Johannes Hospital in Bonn, specializing in general surgery and anesthesia. After 34 months, he continued his education in traumatology at Frankfurt University.

In 1980, Professor Mehrabi founded the first Pediatric Surgery Academy in Iran at Bahrami Pediatric Hospital, setting the foundation for pediatric surgery in the country. Through his dedication, the specialized pediatric surgery department has trained 120 pediatric surgeons to date.

Professor Mehrabi’s illustrious career is decorated with numerous awards and recognitions, earning him the title “Father of Pediatric Surgery” in acknowledgment of his invaluable contributions to the field.

His extensive experience is encapsulated in his authored works, including the 10-volume Illustrated History of Medicine in the World and his comprehensive book on pediatric surgery.

For over 50 years, Professor Mehrabi has upheld his unwavering principle that “a surgeon must be the judge of their own conscience,” saving countless children’s lives and offering hope to their families.

As part of Abadis Med’s commitment to social responsibility, the company honors and celebrates the contributions of distinguished figures in medicine. On Thursday, November 9, 2023, Abadis Med, a supporter of children and medical pioneers, participated in the tribute ceremony for Dr. Valiollah Mehrabi, held at Dr. Fakhreddin Ghavami Hall, Bahrami Children’s Hospital.

This event brought together esteemed professors and medical veterans to express their gratitude to Professor Mehrabi, the father of pediatric surgery in Iran. Abadis Med remains dedicated to its mission of making a meaningful impact.
