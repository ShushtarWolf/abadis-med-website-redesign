Suction Tube

The Abadis Suction Tube is an essential component in medical suction systems, designed for the removal of blood, secretions, and fluids during surgical and therapeutic procedures. Its use enhances the surgeon’s visual clarity and, by enabling rapid fluid evacuation, helps prevent contamination and the spread of infection to surrounding tissues.

Uses of suction tube:

  • Maintaining wound cleanliness for precise visualization of the bleeding site and prevention of infection transfer

  • Draining and cleaning abscess cavities

  • Suitable for thoracic, abdominal, and internal cavity surgeries

  • Effective for evacuating damaged brain tissue or soft brain tumors

  • Assisting in compressing a bleeding vessel

  • Applicable in aesthetic procedures: liposuction, lipolaser, lipomatic

  • Suitable for dental clinics

  • Compatible with fingertip suction controllers, suction catheters, and other related devices

Features and Advantages:

  • Manufactured from medical-grade PVC with high safety standards

  • Sterile, suitable for operating rooms and specialized departments

  • Available in two models:

    • Transparent (for visual monitoring of fluid flow)

    • Ribbed (to prevent kinking and blockage)

  • Controlled flexibility to minimize trauma to sensitive tissues

  • Secure, leak-free connection to various suction devices

  • Compatible with hospital and clinical suction systems

  • Smooth inner surface to prevent clogging and ensure consistent fluid flow

Sizes, Color Coding, and Customization:

Abadis Suction Tubes are available in three standard sizes:

  • 25 – Blue

  • 30 – Gray

  • 35 – Black

Standard Length:

  • 2 meters

Customization Options:

  • Production in different lengths based on the requirements of medical facilities

  • Available in special sizes upon customer request

Product Packaging:

  • Packed in 25-piece die-cut bundles

  • Quantity per carton: 100 pieces

  • Carton dimensions: 58 × 42 × 41 cm

  • Durable packaging suitable for storage and transportation

Fingertip

The Fingertip suction interruption is a small yet highly functional component used in medical suction systems, enabling healthcare professionals to precisely control the level of suction. This device is placed between the suction tubing and other suction accessories such as Nelaton catheters and suction catheters, providing accurate and adjustable performance.

Features & Benefits:

  • Optimal suction control simply by placing or removing a finger from the control port

  • Ergonomic design for long-term use

  • Reduced risk of soft tissue damage during suctioning

  • Compatible with various types of suction tubes

  • Secure connection to suction tubing, preventing fluid leakage

  • Easy-to-use mechanism for quick and effective suction adjustment without turning the suction device on or off

  • Medical-grade construction, suitable for general surgery, dental procedures, and critical care

Material & Sizes:

The fingertip is made of medical-grade polyvinyl chloride (PVC) and is available in two sizes—large and small—to fit different types of suction tubing.

It is widely used in general surgery, plastic surgery, neurosurgery, and dentistry, helping improve suction efficiency while reducing the risk of injury to delicate tissues.
