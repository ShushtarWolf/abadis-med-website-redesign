Method, data source and limits

Formula (surgeries n)
water = n × 1.6 × 9.3 L · cost = n × 2.5 × unit cost · staff time = n × 1.7 × 2.2 × 7.1 ÷ 60 h
Formula (beds: OR a, ICU|CCU b)
water = (254a + 67b) × 1.6 × 9.3 · cost = (200a + 50b) × 2.5 × unit cost · time = (254a + 67b) × 1.7 × 2.2 × 7.1 ÷ 60
Data source
Coefficients are taken unchanged from the calculator on abadis-med.com; the Iranian unit cost is 85,000 IRT. [Scientific basis of the coefficients: pending from the company]
Assumptions & limits
An estimate of savings versus washing and disinfecting reusable canisters. Exchange rates are not applied: choose IRT or enter your own local unit cost. Local water tariffs, wages and cleaning protocols change the result.
