The Presence Of Abadis At Uzmedexpo

UzMedExpo is an important annual event in the healthcare industry, specifically tailored to the needs of Uzbekistan and the broader Central Asian region. This healthcare exhibition serves as a vital platform for professionals, stakeholders, and companies operating in the medical and healthcare sectors to come together, exchange knowledge, and showcase the latest innovations and advancements in the field. UzMedExpo provides an opportunity for networking, collaboration, and learning about cutting-edge technologies, pharmaceuticals, medical equipment, and healthcare solutions. It plays a crucial role in fostering advancements in healthcare delivery, improving patient care, and contributing to the overall well-being of the population in the region. This event exemplifies the commitment to enhancing healthcare infrastructure and services, ultimately benefiting the people of Uzbekistan and the surrounding nations.

It is worth noting that Abadis Medical Reservoirs, as the first manufacturer of Disposable Suction Bags in the Middle East, will be participating in the 15th International Medical Equipment Exhibition of Uzbekistan, which is scheduled to take place from September 20th to 22nd, 2023, in Tashkent, Uzbekistan.

Place: Iran Pavilion

Date: 20th to 22nd September 2023
