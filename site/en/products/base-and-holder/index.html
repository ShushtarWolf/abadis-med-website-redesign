Plastic Suction Canister Clamp

The Abadis Suction Canister Clamp​ is designed for the secure installation and support of a suction canister on a wall or hospital equipment console. Manufactured from durable plastic, it keeps the canister securely in place while providing healthcare personnel with convenient access to the suction system.

The Abadis Wall-Mounted Suction Canister Holder is washable at temperatures of up to 95°C and is suitable for use in hospitals, operating rooms, intensive care units, and other healthcare facilities.

Product Name Product Code
Plastic Suction Canister Clamp 060503

Rear part of Plastic Suction Canister Clamp

The Abadis Rear part of  Suction Canister Clamp is positioned behind and connected to the wall-mounted suction canister holder, enabling the suction canister to be securely installed on hospital portable suction units. Manufactured from durable plastic, this component helps keep the canister firmly in place and prevents unwanted movement during operation or transportation of the unit.

The Abadis Portable Suction Canister Rear Bracket is washable at temperatures of up to 95°C and is suitable for use in hospitals and other healthcare facilities
