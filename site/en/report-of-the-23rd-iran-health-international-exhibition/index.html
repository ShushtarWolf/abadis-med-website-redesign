Report of the 23rd Iran Health International Exhibition

The 23rd Iran Health International Exhibition of medical, dental, laboratory and pharmaceutical equipment was held at the permanent venue of Tehran International Exhibition on  May 24–27, 2022 from 9:00 to 16:00. More than 24,000 people visited this exhibition.

Abadis Med. also participated in this exhibition. Abadis booth in hall 38 was visited by more than 5000 visitors. Abadis was able to get very good results in this exhibition, and this means that Abadis can be of great help to medical centers and universities of medical sciences in the issues related to hospital infection control.

During the exhibition, a large number of visitors from all across Iran, as well as visitors from Russia, Armenia, Iraq, Syria, Turkey and Georgia, came to Abadis booth.

In the following, you will read a report about what different news agencies have said and written about Abadis, as well as about notable people who visited the Abadis stand.

Prominent people who visited Abadis booth:

On the first day of the 23rd International Iran Health Exhibition, Mr. Seyyed Mehdi Niazi, Deputy Director of Planning ِDepartment of the Ministry of Industry, Mining and Trade, participated in the exhibition and visited Abadis booth. He pointed out that Abadis is the youngest medical equipment company in the country with over 30 employees.

 

 

Also, Mr. Abdulnaser Azad Bakht, Head of the Office of Medical and Laboratory Equipment Industry, accompanied Mr. Seyyed Mehdi Niazi in visiting Abadis’ booth and visited Abadis products.

 

 

Dr. Majid Rohi, President of the Association of Manufacturers and Exporters of Medical, Dental and Laboratory Equipment, along with Dr. Sorena Satari, Vice President for Science and Technology under president Hassan Rouhani, participated in the exhibition and visited the products of Abadis Med.

On the 4th day of the 23rd International Iran Health Exhibition, Mr. Bahram Daraei, the head of the Food and Drug Organization, attended the exhibition and visited the booth and products of Abadis Med. The issue of waste management and new ideas by Abadis was welcomed by Mr. Daraei.

 

 

Accompanied with Mr. Bahram daraei, Mr. Ruhollah Mazinani, head of the General Department of Medical Equipment and Necessities, also visited Abadis booth and its products.

 

 

On the last day of the Iran Health exhibition, Dr. Asif Mahdavi Ardakani (Secretary of the Health Products Export Policy Council of the Food and Drug Organization) along with Mr. Bahram Daraei and Mr. Ruhollah Mazinani visited the Abadis booth.

 

 

Abadis Med., the largest manufacturer of disposable suction bags in Iran, has deep operations in hospital infection control. This company has been able to collect and manage 5 million liters of hospital infections during its 5 years of operation. In addition, this company has managed to save 5 million liters of water.
Currently, more than 5000 consumers are using Abadis products. In this regard, the 5th anniversary celebration of Abadis Med. was held on June 26, 2022 at Abadis booth in Iran Health Exhibition.

What is Abadis Med Co. challenge of the 5+5 million effect?

Abadis was able to collect 5 million hospital infections from the country’s medical centers during its 5 years of operation. This company was also able to save 5 million liters of water.

Now the question is, what amounts can this savings be equivalent to?

  • 5 million liters of infection is equivalent to 3 million and 333 thousand drinking bottles. This amount is 720 times more than “per capita consumption of beverages in one year” in Iran.
  • 5 million liters of infection is equivalent to one and a half times the size of Iran’s biggest stadium, “Azadi”.
  • With 5 million liters of water, 2 hectares of wheat ground can be cultivated per year.
  • 1000 tons of steel products can be produced with 5 million liters of water.
