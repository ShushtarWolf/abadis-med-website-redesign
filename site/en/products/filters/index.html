Antibacterial filter

The antibacterial (viral) filter sits between the suction hose and the canister. It stops microorganisms, germs and bacteria from travelling toward the suction source, so the surroundings are not contaminated by the microorganisms in the suction fluid. It filters microorganisms down to 0.03 microns. This limits the spread of dangerous infections in the intensive-care unit or operating room and greatly reduces the patient’s risk of hospital-acquired infection. To prevent transmission, replace the filter after the patient is discharged.

Uses

  • Fits portable and hospital suction units.
  • Protects the patient from bacteria and contamination.

Advantages

  • Stops microorganisms spreading into the surrounding area.
  • Filters germs and bacteria at 99.99%.
  • The filter is easy to see and to change.

Service life

  • Replace the filter when the paper inside changes colour. Changing it every six months gives the best performance.

Flow-stopper filter (shutoff)

Disposable suction bags include a filter that stops suctioned fluid from overflowing. These hydrophobic filters are an important barrier against contaminated fluid: when liquid reaches the filter, suction stops and the suction unit is protected.

The inner filter is one of the porous filters made by Abadis. The frame of the flow-stopper (shutoff) filter is polycarbonate.

Uses

  • Fits portable and hospital suction units.
  • Prevents suctioned fluid from overflowing.
  • Prevents contaminated and infectious fluid from spreading.

Advantages

  • Stops toxic gases from electrosurgery and electrocautery smoke from escaping.
  • Protects the suction unit and extends its life.

Service life

  • Replace the filter when liquid reaches its surface or suction pressure drops. For correct operation, replace the flow-stopper filter every month.

Porous filter

Porous filters are widely used in medical and laboratory equipment. They are made of ultra-high-molecular-weight polyethylene (UHMW-PE).

As soon as the pores are wetted, the polymer grains swell and close, and liquid cannot pass.

Features

  • High, stable filtration
  • Fast action
  • Bacterial filtration of 99.99%
