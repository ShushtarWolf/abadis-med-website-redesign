T connector

T connector balances the flow and vacuum between the suction bag and the canister to prevent the suction bag from collapsing upwards.

Usage:

In 2 and 3 liter canisters, due to the larger volume, we use the T connector to exchange the vacuum inside the bag and the canister.

Material:

Tconnector is made of Polyacetal Polyacetal.

030402 T connector

Connector

It is an adapter that attaches to the warhead of the suction bag and creates a vacuum through the connection of a silicone slang to the Tconnector.
