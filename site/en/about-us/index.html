About us

Abadis Medical is an Iranian knowledge-based company driven by scientific thinking, innovation, and a strong sense of responsibility. As a pioneering and leading manufacturer of specialized infection control products, Abadis has made significant contributions to improving hygiene, safety, and operational efficiency in healthcare facilities.

Built on engineering expertise, advanced technologies, and a deep understanding of healthcare needs, Abadis began its journey with one successful product. Today, with a solution-oriented approach, the company is evolving toward a comprehensive portfolio of specialized solutions for safer and more efficient healthcare.

Our Mission

The mission of Abadis Medical is to provide specialized products and solutions for efficient hospital fluid and secretion management, intelligent and optimized medical waste management, preventing the spread of infections, and enhancing healthcare safety and operational efficiency.

Through scientific expertise, advanced technologies, continuous innovation, intelligent management, and skilled human capital, Abadis is committed to developing sustainable solutions that protect patients and healthcare professionals, reduce operational costs, conserve resources, and safeguard the environment.

Our Vision

The vision of Abadis Medical is to maintain its pioneering and leading position in Iran’s medical device industry while becoming a recognized developer of specialized healthcare solutions in regional and international markets.

As Abadis enters its third five-year chapter of growth and development, the company is advancing from one successful product to a comprehensive portfolio of specialized solutions, guided by three strategic growth pillars:

  • Market Expansion & Exports — Strengthening its leadership in hospital suction systems, expanding its product portfolio, and developing international markets.
  • Specialized Product Development — Designing and commercializing new products for airway safety and care, including suction toothbrushes, endotracheal tube holders, and related medical devices.
  • Future Technologies — Transforming research in advanced materials and PVA technologies into practical solutions and innovative products for contamination control and medical waste management.

Abadis envisions a future built on scientific knowledge, innovation, intelligent management, and sustainable development — where business growth creates a meaningful impact on human health and the environment.

Achievements & Capabilities

As Iran’s first and largest manufacturer of disposable suction bags, Abadis Medical has established a distinguished position in the medical device industry, supported by its manufacturing licensing history with Iran’s Ministry of Industry, Mine and Trade, actual production capabilities, and extensive market presence.

Today, Abadis holds an estimated 90% share of Iran’s domestic disposable suction bag market, maintains sales coverage across 28 provinces, and supplies its diverse product portfolio to more than 1,000 customers, playing a leading role in the development of hospital suction systems.

The company’s comprehensive suction system portfolio includes disposable suction bags, suction tubing, various types of filters, canisters, vacuum regulators, stands, trolleys, connectors, and related accessories.

Modern manufacturing infrastructure, quality control systems, an established distribution network, expanding export operations, and Abadis’ skilled human capital form the foundation of the company’s continuous growth and progress.

Abadis also leverages Business Intelligence (BI), data analytics, and emerging Artificial Intelligence (AI) applications to support smarter management, more efficient operations, greater productivity, and more informed decision-making.

History and Background of Abadis Knowledge-Based Group

Disposable suction bags have been used in developed countries since the 1990s. In Iran, efforts to introduce and promote these products began in 2010 through the management and founders of Abzar Darman Persia.

Their experience in medical equipment importation and supply, understanding of healthcare needs, and belief in domestic manufacturing laid the foundation for the establishment of Abadis Medical in 2017.

Initial Phase — Pioneering Hospital Fluid and Secretion Management

Abadis began by manufacturing disposable suction bags and promoting their adoption across healthcare facilities, becoming Iran’s first and largest manufacturer of these products. This journey continued with the development of comprehensive suction systems, including suction tubing, various filters, canisters, vacuum regulators, and related accessories.

Current Phase — Advancing Airway Safety and Care

Building on its industrial experience, knowledge-based capabilities, and solution-oriented approach, Abadis is developing new specialized products, including suction toothbrushes, endotracheal tube holders, and related medical devices.

Future Phase — Innovation in Material Technologies and Waste Management

Research and development in PVA-based products and advanced material technologies form part of Abadis’ future plans, with the aim of delivering practical and sustainable solutions for contamination control and hospital waste management.

This journey reflects the evolution of Abadis from one successful product to a comprehensive portfolio of specialized solutions in its third five-year chapter of development.

This belief is the foundation of the Abadis Green Family — a community bringing together the company’s human capital, healthcare professionals, distributors, and healthcare partners who share a commitment to advancing healthcare, social responsibility, and environmental protection.

  • Supporting hospital infection control and enhancing the safety of patients and healthcare professionals.
  • Promoting intelligent and efficient medical waste management while reducing contamination risks.
  • Protecting the environment, conserving resources, and operating in line with sustainable development principles.
  • Developing scientific and innovative solutions for safer and more efficient healthcare.
  • Supporting physicians, nurses, medical equipment specialists, and healthcare professionals.

Abadis believes that meaningful impact begins with small actions. Together with the Abadis Green Family, these actions can contribute to a healthier and more sustainable future.

Our Goals

With a scientific mindset, intelligent management, high quality standards, and continuous innovation, Abadis pursues its objectives through sustainable growth, technological development, and meaningful impact.

  • Market Leadership & Exports — Maintaining its leading position in hospital suction systems, expanding international markets, and establishing long-term business partnerships.
  • Product & Solution Development — Expanding its product portfolio and developing specialized solutions for patient safety, infection control, and hospital fluid management.
  • Research & Development — Transforming scientific knowledge and advanced material technologies, including PVA, into practical products and innovative, sustainable waste management solutions.
  • Intelligent Management — Advancing Business Intelligence, data analytics, and Artificial Intelligence applications to improve productivity, decision-making, and organizational efficiency.
  • Human Capital & Abadis Academy — Developing educational programs and establishing Abadis Academy to empower human capital, enhance the knowledge and skills of medical device professionals, and contribute to the advancement of the industry.
  • Sustainability & Social Responsibility — Conserving resources, protecting the environment, and strengthening a culture of meaningful impact and shared responsibility within the Abadis Green Family.
