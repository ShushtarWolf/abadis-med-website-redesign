Report of the International Medical Equipment Exhibition of Uzbekistan 2023

The 15th International Exhibition of Medical and Pharmaceutical Equipment of Uzbekistan was held from 20 to 22 September 2023 in Tashkent, and 27 Iranian companies, including 15 knowledge-based companies, participated in Uzmedexpo.

By participating in this exhibition, Abadis Med Knowledge Base Company, in addition to presenting its products by equipping two medical centers and getting to know the distribution companies, was able to take the initial steps to have a wide presence and equipped all medical centers and hospitals in Uzbekistan.

On this subject, meetings were held with the Technology Park of Tashkent, as well as regular meetings with the Ministry of Health and the Economic Counselor of the Iranian Embassy in Uzbekistan.

Hopefully we will be able to raise Iran’s flag in the field of infection control and environmental health by being present in surrounding countries.
