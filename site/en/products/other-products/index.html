Suction Brush

The Suction toothbrush is a special type of toothbrush that is often used in medical patients who are unable to maintain proper oral hygiene. The suction toothbrush can be purchased with a head made of hair or a swab. The head then uses a slang to vacuum to provide the power to suck up debris and particles released during the toothbrush. Studies have shown that regular use has health benefits.

Multi-Purpose Surgical Floor Suction (Splash-Vac)

The multi-purpose surgical floor suction device is a specialized medical tool designed for the efficient collection of fluids, hair, fat, and pathological waste from the floor in sterile environments such as operating rooms, surgical clinics, and critical care units.

This system connects directly to central or portable suction units, enabling hands-free floor cleaning during and after surgery. By preventing the spread of contaminants around the surgical area, it supports infection control protocols and improves operational safety.

 Benefits:

  • Rapid and complete removal of large volumes of surgical fluids from floors in ORs and clinics

  • Flexible suction hose for easier handling and positioning

  • Compatible with both central and portable suction systems

  • Minimizes manual cleanup, helping reduce the risk of contamination and save staff time
