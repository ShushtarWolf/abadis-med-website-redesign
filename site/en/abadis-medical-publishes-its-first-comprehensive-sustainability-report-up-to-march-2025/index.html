Abadis Medical Publishes Its First Comprehensive Sustainability Report (Up to March 2025)

Abadis Medical Co., a leading knowledge-based manufacturer of sustainable medical devices in Iran, has officially released its first comprehensive Sustainability Report covering achievements up to March 2025.

Aligned with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (UN SDGs), the report presents Abadis’s strategic actions and measurable outcomes across Good Health and Well-being (SDG 3), Clean Water and Sanitation (SDG 6), Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure (SDG 9), Responsible Consumption and Production (SDG 12), and Climate Action (SDG 13).

Key highlights include:

  • Development of sustainable and infection-safe products in the healthcare sector
  • Elimination of printed catalogs through the No Paper Exhibition initiative
  • Large-scale oak tree planting in the Zagros region as part of the Green Zagros Project
  • Implementation of a Business Intelligence (BI) system to monitor sustainability indicators
  • Creation of green jobs and empowerment of skilled personnel

By the end of March 2025, the use of Abadis disposable suction liner bags has resulted in over 38 million liters of water saved, a significant reduction in infectious waste generation, and improved hospital safety across Iran and the Middle East.

This publication reinforces Abadis Medical’s ongoing commitment to sustainable healthcare, environmental protection, and responsible industrial growth.
