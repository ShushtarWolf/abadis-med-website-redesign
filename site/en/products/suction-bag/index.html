Disposable suction bag

Abadis suction bags are made in 1-, 2- and 3-litre volumes, with hydrophobic, dual and mechanical filtration.

Shared features: gelling powder, overflow protection, compatibility with suction units, and protection of the central suction system.

Fitted singly or in series on a fixed (wall) base and on a portable base. Installation and after-sales service are free.

Hydrophobic filter

Stops fluid overflow and protects the pump and the central suction system.

Gelling powder

Turns liquid into a gel so it is safer to carry and less likely to leak.

Full compatibility

Compatible with portable and central suction units.
